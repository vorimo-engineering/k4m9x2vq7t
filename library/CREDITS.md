# The samplers' factory sounds — where each one comes from

Made by `scripts/make_sampler_library.py` and `web/match/make_library.mjs`; every file here is a recording below, trimmed, levelled and encoded as FLAC (`<id>.flac`, the id a hash of its PCM). `<id>.psola.f32` and `<id>.resynth.f32` are the Grain sampler's and the Resynth's measurements of it, made with the MATCH tab's own builders.

Every sound here may ship in the device as a factory preset (Volo, 2026-11-13: licences "similar to MIT"): CC0, CC BY with the credit line below, or YouTone's own — rendered by its synth engine from Volo's own presets. Nothing is a recording of a manufacturer's sample ROM.
